Essentials

Core community engagement tools.

Welcome

Send automated join/leave messages and auto-roles. Customize your server's first impression with beautiful welcome cards and embeds.

Commands

Configure internal server slash commands. Manage permissions, cooldowns, and custom command aliases for your community.

Reaction Roles

Self-assignable roles via emoji reactions. Let members choose their own roles, colors, and access levels with a single click.

Moderator

Advanced manual and auto moderation tools. Keep your server safe with word filters, link blocking, and powerful staff commands.

Levels

XP and leveling system with role rewards. Encourage active participation by rewarding your most engaged members with special ranks.

Achievements

Reward user milestones with badges. Track long-term progress and unlock unique digital collectibles for consistent server activity.

Leaderboard

Track XP rankings in a clean table view. Show off your top members and foster healthy competition within your community.

Announcements

Schedule future messages or embeds. Plan your server updates and community events in advance with automated posting.

Pro Suite

Deep analytics and AI tools.

Health

Hourly rollups and activity trends. Monitor your server's growth, peak activity hours, and overall community engagement metrics.

Ranking Pro

Time-sliced advanced rankings. Deep dive into user performance with daily, weekly, and monthly activity filters.

AI Mod

Semantic DeepSeek toxicity filtering. Use state-of-the-art AI to detect harassment and spam that regular word filters miss.

Network

D3.js social interaction visualizer. Explore the complex web of mentions and replies to find the true influencers in your server.

Profile

Member heatmaps and badge grids. Give users a professional portfolio of their server contributions and earned achievements.
